Permissions sit at the top because they are the one setting that silently ruins a recording. Everything about where your words go is stated in the setting itself, not in a policy page.

Two typefaces

Monospace is what you typed; the serif is what the meeting wrote back. Both stay on screen so you can see which words were yours.

Timestamps lead

The moment you typed a fragment is how it finds the right stretch of transcript, so it earns the left margin rather than decorating it.

Colour means live

One amber, used only for audio that is actually being captured. At rest the app has no colour at all.

Every claim checkable

Open “What was said” on any point to read the transcript either side of the moment you typed. The note never asks to be taken on trust.
